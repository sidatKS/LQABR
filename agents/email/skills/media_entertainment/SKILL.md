# Outreach email — media & entertainment

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at a media, entertainment, publishing, arts or sports business.
Creative-industry pace, receives a lot of cold email, and can tell instantly
when something was mass-produced. Assume they read the first sentence and
decide from it.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound leads. We score real
engagement — opens, clicks, replies — rather than form fills, so a rep's day
starts with the people who actually responded instead of a queue sorted by
arrival time.

## The problem to lead with

Inbound enquiries — advertisers, partners, licensing, sponsorship — arrive
faster than a small team can work them, and the ordering is arrival time rather
than interest. Frame this as a general pattern in the sector, not a diagnosis
of their business, which you know nothing about.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills.

## Additional restraint for this sector

Say nothing about their content, titles, audiences, ratings, rights or
creative work. This email is about their inbound enquiries.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the enquiry-backlog problem as a sector pattern.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — concrete, unhyped.
4. The call to action as a link to a short overview.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
