# Outreach email — retail & consumer goods

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at a retailer, wholesaler or consumer-goods brand. Fast-moving,
margin-conscious, and used to filtering a lot of cold email. Assume they read
the first sentence and decide from it.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound enquiries, scoring real
engagement rather than form fills, so the commercial team works the enquiries
that showed genuine interest first.

## The problem to lead with

Inbound enquiries — wholesale accounts, partnerships, trade interest — arrive
faster than a small team can work them, and the ordering is arrival time rather
than interest. Frame it as a general pattern in the sector, not a diagnosis of
their business, which you know nothing about.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills.

## Additional restraint for this sector

Say nothing about their products, ranges, stores, stock, pricing, margins or
customers. This email is about their inbound enquiries, not their catalogue.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the enquiry-backlog problem as a sector pattern.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — concrete, unhyped.
4. The call to action as a link to a short overview.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
