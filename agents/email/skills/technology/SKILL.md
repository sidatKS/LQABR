# Outreach email — technology

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at a software, SaaS or IT services company. Technical or
technically fluent, receives a lot of cold email, and can tell instantly when
something was mass-produced. Assume they read the first sentence and decide
from it.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound leads. We score real
engagement — opens, clicks, replies — rather than form fills, so a rep's day
starts with the people who actually responded instead of a queue sorted by
arrival time.

## The problem to lead with

Inbound volume grows faster than the headcount qualifying it. Good leads sit
behind bad ones in an undifferentiated queue and go cold while someone works
the list in order. Frame this as an observation about how software teams tend
to operate — not as a diagnosis of their own pipeline, which you know
nothing about.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills. That is the whole offer.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the queue problem, tied to their ROLE where the
   supplied facts support it. You have no company name — do not reach for one.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — concrete, unhyped.
4. The call to action as a link, offering something small: a short overview,
   not a demo or a meeting.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
