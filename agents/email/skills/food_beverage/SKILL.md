# Outreach email — food, beverage & agriculture

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at a food or beverage producer, grower, distributor or grocer.
Practical, volume-driven, relationship-based selling, and no patience for
software marketing language. Plain speech lands; jargon does not.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound enquiries, scoring real
engagement rather than form fills, so the sales team follows up on the
enquiries worth a conversation.

## The problem to lead with

Enquiries come in from buyers, distributors, trade shows and the website, and
someone works through them by hand in the order they landed. The serious buyer
and the casual enquiry get the same treatment. Frame it as a familiar pattern
in the sector.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills.

## Additional restraint for this sector

Say nothing about their products, ingredients, recipes, sourcing, food safety,
supply chain or shelf life. You know nothing about their operations and this
email is about their inbound enquiries.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the by-hand enquiry problem as a sector pattern.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — plain, no software jargon.
4. The call to action as a link to a short overview.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
