# Outreach email — construction & built environment

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at a construction firm, civil engineer, architecture practice,
building-materials supplier, developer or facilities business. Relationship-
driven, project-paced, and allergic to software marketing language. Plain
speech lands; jargon does not.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound enquiries, scoring real
engagement rather than form fills, so the team follows up on the enquiries
worth a quote or a meeting.

## The problem to lead with

Enquiries come in from the website, referrals and tender lists, and someone
works through them by hand in the order they arrived. The serious prospect and
the tyre-kicker get the same treatment. Frame it as a familiar pattern in the
sector.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills.

## Additional restraint for this sector

Say nothing about their projects, sites, buildings, tenders, permits,
materials, timelines or safety. You know nothing about their work and this
email is about their inbound enquiries, not their portfolio.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the by-hand enquiry problem as a sector pattern.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — plain, no software jargon.
4. The call to action as a link to a short overview.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
