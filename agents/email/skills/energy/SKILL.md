# Outreach email — energy, utilities & resources

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at an energy, utility, oil & gas, renewables, mining or metals
company. Capital-intensive, long cycles, procurement-heavy, and deeply
unimpressed by software marketing. Plain speech lands; anything that sounds
like a pitch does not.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound enquiries, scoring real
engagement rather than form fills, so the commercial team follows up on the
enquiries worth a conversation.

## The problem to lead with

Enquiries arrive from the website, events and partners, and someone works
through them by hand in the order they landed. The serious counterparty and
the casual researcher get the same treatment. Frame it as a familiar pattern
in the sector.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills.

## Additional restraint for this sector

Say nothing about their operations, plants, grid, reserves, extraction,
production, capacity, commodity prices, emissions, safety or any regulatory
regime. You know nothing about their operations and this email is about their
inbound enquiries, not their assets.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the by-hand enquiry problem as a sector pattern.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — plain, no software jargon.
4. The call to action as a link to a short overview.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
