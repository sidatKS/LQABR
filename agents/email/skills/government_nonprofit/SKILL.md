# Outreach email — government, public sector & nonprofit

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at a government body, public-sector organisation, nonprofit,
association or civic institution. Careful about vendors, accountable, and
unimpressed by enthusiasm. Credibility matters more than energy here.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound enquiries, scoring real
engagement rather than form fills, so the team follows up on the enquiries that
showed genuine interest first.

## The problem to lead with

Enquiries arrive from the website, events and partners faster than a small team
can work them, and the ordering is arrival time rather than interest. Frame it
as a general pattern.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills.

## Additional restraint for this sector

Take no political position and imply none. Say nothing about their mandate,
policy, constituents, donors, beneficiaries, funding or mission beyond the fact
that they field inbound enquiries. This email is about that enquiry flow and
nothing else.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the enquiry-backlog problem as a general pattern.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — plain, measured.
4. The call to action as a link to a short overview.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
