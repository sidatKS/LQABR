# Outreach email — manufacturing

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at a manufacturer or industrial supplier. Long sales cycles,
relationship-driven business, and a strong allergy to software marketing
language. Plain speech lands; jargon does not.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound enquiries, scoring real
engagement rather than form fills, so the sales team follows up on the
enquiries worth a quote.

## The problem to lead with

Enquiries come in from distributors, trade shows and the website, and someone
works through them by hand in the order they landed. The serious buyer and the
student doing research get the same treatment. Frame it as a familiar pattern
in industrial sales.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills.

## Additional restraint for this sector

Say nothing about their production, plant, equipment, capacity, lead times,
tariffs or supply chain. You know nothing about their operations and this
email is about their inbound enquiries, not their factory.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the by-hand enquiry problem as an industry pattern.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — plain, no software jargon.
4. The call to action as a link to a short overview.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
