# Outreach email — hospitality, travel & leisure

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at a hospitality, travel, restaurant, events or leisure business.
Fast-moving, service-focused, and used to filtering a lot of cold email. Assume
they read the first sentence and decide from it.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound enquiries, scoring real
engagement rather than form fills, so the team follows up on the enquiries
worth a conversation first.

## The problem to lead with

Enquiries — bookings, groups, partnerships, events — arrive from the website,
partners and referrals faster than a small team can work them, and the ordering
is arrival time rather than interest. Frame it as a familiar pattern in the
sector.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills.

## Additional restraint for this sector

Say nothing about their venues, rooms, menus, guests, occupancy or service.
This email is about their inbound enquiries, not their operation.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the enquiry-backlog problem as a sector pattern.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — concrete, unhyped.
4. The call to action as a link to a short overview.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
