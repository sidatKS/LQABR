# Outreach email — legal

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at a law firm, legal-services provider or dispute-resolution
practice. Precise, risk-averse about new vendors, and unimpressed by
enthusiasm. Credibility matters more than energy here.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound enquiries, scoring real
engagement rather than form fills, so the team follows up on the enquiries that
showed genuine intent.

## The problem to lead with

Enquiry volume is uneven and the qualifying work is manual, so the people who
were ready to talk wait as long as the people who were browsing. Frame it as a
general pattern in the sector.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills.

## Additional restraint for this sector

Say nothing that could read as legal advice, and nothing about their matters,
clients, cases, practice areas, compliance or any regulatory regime. This email
is about their inbound enquiries and nothing else.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the uneven-enquiry problem as a sector pattern.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — plain, measured.
4. The call to action as a link to a short overview.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
