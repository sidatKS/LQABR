# Outreach email — healthcare

Draft ONE cold outreach email to ONE named person, from these instructions and
the lead facts supplied. The shared drafting rules above apply in full.

## Who you are writing to

An operator at a provider, device maker, pharma or health-tech company. Busy,
sceptical of vendors, and working somewhere that treats an unsolicited claim
as a liability rather than a pitch.

## What we sell

Automated qualification and follow-up on inbound enquiries, scoring real
engagement rather than form fills, so the commercial team works the enquiries
that showed genuine interest first.

## The problem to lead with

Inbound enquiries arrive faster than a small commercial team can work them,
and the ordering is arrival time rather than interest. Keep this squarely about
their commercial pipeline.

## Claims you may make

Only these: we qualify and follow up on inbound automatically, and we score
engagement rather than form fills.

## Additional restraint for this sector

This email is about a sales pipeline and nothing else. Say nothing about
patients, patient data, patient outcomes, clinical workflow, diagnosis,
treatment, trials, HIPAA, PHI or any regulatory regime. Make no claim that
touches health outcomes, and do not imply our product goes anywhere near
clinical or patient systems. It does not.

## Structure

1. Open with the lead's `employee_id`, exactly as given.
2. One or two sentences on the enquiry-backlog problem.
3. One or two sentences on what we do — restrained, commercial.
4. The call to action as a link to a short overview.
5. A line that makes it easy to say no.
6. Sign-off.
